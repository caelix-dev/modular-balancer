---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-11-18
  Features:
    - Item filters per block, in the lower part of the settings window: five slots (the block's outputs take only these items, and these items go only to blocks that list them), Exclude (everything but these items), and Per lane (slot 1 on the left lane, slot 2 on the right lane). The whole balancer routes by them with filter splitters in its hidden network, no script.
    - Filters travel with copy and paste settings, blueprints, ghosts, clones and block upgrades.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.5
Date: 2026-11-18
  Features:
    - Balancer settings (balance lanes, redistribute unused shares) can be set on block ghosts: click a ghost to open the settings, or copy and paste settings onto or from a ghost. The settings go to every connected ghost of the same tier and apply when they are built; a ghost next to a built balancer shows and changes that balancer's settings. An open settings window follows its ghost to the block built in its place.
  Locale:
    - The message for inserters on a block that already uses its tile now also names circuit-wired belts.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.4
Date: 2026-11-10
  Changes:
    - Every balancer block tier now weighs 20 kg, so a rocket carries 50 (one stack). Before, the weight came from the recipe: 28 per rocket, and 14 for turbo blocks.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.3
Date: 2026-10-30
  Bugfixes:
    - A belt dragged across a balancer's input or output belt replaced that belt and merged the two lines. The last belt tile next to a block is now an obstacle to belts crossing it: a dragged belt goes under it with underground belts. Dragging a belt along the line in its direction still replaces it (to upgrade it); a single belt can no longer be clicked onto it.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.2
Date: 2026-10-27
  Bugfixes:
    - A balancer block ghost placed on a belt (for example a forced ghost build with shift) no longer makes construction robots carry the block back: the belt is marked for deconstruction and the block replaces it, keeping the belt and its items.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.1
Date: 2026-10-27
  Changes:
    - Characters and vehicles can move over balancer blocks, as over belts. Blocks still cannot overlap buildings, inserters, rails, trees, water or other blocks.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-27
  Features:
    - Modular N:M belt balancer: place 1x1 blocks in any connected shape and attach belts; every belt feeding a block is an input, every belt it feeds is an output.
    - Exact equal outputs, inputs drawn equally under backpressure, no items lost or duplicated, no per-tick script work in the steady state.
    - Per-balancer settings: balance lanes, redistribute unused shares. Copy-paste settings and blueprints keep them.
    - Four tiers matching the vanilla belts (turbo with or without Space Age); blueprints, undo/redo, ghosts and the upgrade planner work.
